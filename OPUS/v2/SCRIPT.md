## If you’re running long
- Drop the bracketed words (about 4 s).
- Tamagotchi: skip the words on **Bliss** — just click through it.
- Calendar: say only “Polytrauma. Re-planned in milliseconds. Tonight, 22:20.”
- Never cut the outro.

## Delivery
- **Light part:** fast and playful. Let each face land, then one line. The faces do the comedy.
- **The TV dies:** this is the turn. Silence until “Now.” appears. Don’t rush it.
- **Dark part:** slower, lower voice. Point at the screen once per beat: the swarm, the gold, the alarm, the phone.
- On “in milliseconds”, the caption shows the real measured re-plan time.
- In the outro, slow down by 20 %. Pause before “Nobody babysits” and before “We’re Aloha Robotics”.

## Controls
