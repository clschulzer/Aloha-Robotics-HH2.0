Hospital schedule feeds, identity and authorization, manufacturer service interfaces, validated device checks, robot docking and a real agent runtime. No hospital, model, robot or medical device is connected.

Planning reserve is an illustrative assumption, not a guaranteed rollback duration. Mechanical calibration, motion and radiation remain outside this demonstrator.
