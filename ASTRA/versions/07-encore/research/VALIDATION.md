# Validation — The Care Loop

Tested in installed headless Firefox at 1440 × 810, with a real three-minute playback clock.

## Complete performance

- All 31 timed cues arrived in order. The presentation reached 180 seconds and held the final frame.
- The complete original 30.442667-second movie played with its soundtrack. Ordinary next-cue navigation could not skip it.
- The scouts called the actual candidate evaluator more than a thousand times during the run.
- The calculated Cio window remained 21:00–23:00, with recovery margin to 23:30 and Cora as backup.
- The phone, start sequence and final product state received the same calculated proposal.
- Exactly one illustrated human approval was recorded. None was recorded before the demonstrated decision.
- No browser console errors occurred.

The final title renderer caches the formed lettering and its glow instead of blurring the same text on every frame. The complete performance was replayed after this change. The receipts retain observed frame rates; they describe this headless environment, not a guarantee for every computer.

## Layout, motion and controls

The complete cue inspection checked image availability, primary layout boundaries, correct embedded product steps, previous navigation, notes, sound, pause and reset. The embedded progress timer stopped and resumed with the parent presentation.

Motion captures cover the character transitions, priority reveal, cancellation, candidate search, golden-window convergence, phone handoff, mission phases, human decision, three-pillar growth and close. A targeted introduction capture checks that the title settles before the next cue.

A separate delivery check exercised all seven presenter channels. Next and previous controls operated the matching stage, and the current title and speaker text agreed. Longer technical notes initially pushed those controls below the window; the presenter now reserves room for navigation and lets long notes scroll.

All 20 embedded final-product cues passed additional layout-pair checks. The gallery was also checked at desktop and phone widths, with all seven covers loaded and no horizontal overflow.

## Executable scenario and source checks

Seven final-story checks pass:

1. The cancellation opens an eligible Cio window.
2. The displayed result is Cio’s highest-ranked eligible window.
3. The backup remains free through the recovery margin.
4. An emergency hold removes the proposal.
5. Missing confirming staff prevents a proposal.
6. Missing authorization blocks the start gate.
7. A recalled package blocks the start.

The original planner and scorer suites pass **27/27** tests. The copied rule core, scorer and synthetic-day source match the repository originals by SHA-256.

The product’s mission, camera, docking, approval tap, installation progress and receipt are simulations. The browser sends no device command. Production authorization, clinical release and physical safety still belong to the proposed validated integration.
