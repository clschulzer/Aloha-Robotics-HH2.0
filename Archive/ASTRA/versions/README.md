## What I would rehearse first

**[07 — The Care Loop](07-encore/)** is my recommendation for the main pitch. It follows Christian’s actual product journey, gives the jokes room, and carries one consistent example from the calendar to the service receipt. The [223-word script](07-encore/SCRIPT.md) leaves time for the audience to watch.

Choose **[01 — Ember](01-ember/)** if you want the tighter character → calendar → architecture structure from your original brief. Choose **[04 — Constellation](04-constellation/)** for the largest motion performance and visible emergency replanning. **[05 — Attachment Issues](05-attachment-issues/)** puts the comedy furthest forward.

**[02 — In Good Hands](02-christian/)** retains the earlier, complete Christian-template interpretation. **[03 — Night Shift](03-night-shift/)** is the cinematic alternative. **[06 — Open Circuit](06-open-circuit/)** is the technical presentation and the best source for engineering Q&A; it deliberately uses the full three minutes without the movie.

These are independent alternatives. Their assets, code, spoken scripts and review records remain available in their own folders.

## Rehearsal

The gallery opens each stage directly. **Watch 3-minute preview** runs the entire presentation; **Present live** gives control to the clicker. The final frame holds at 180 seconds. The entertainment versions preserve the original complete film and its audio.

[Presenter window](presenter.html?v=01-ember): replace the `v` query value with the desired folder name. Keep it in the same browser as the stage. It shows the current script, next cue and elapsed time and can advance the stage.

The calendar records are synthetic. “Built” refers to the browser prototype and deterministic planner; production hospital and hardware integrations remain proposed. Speaker notes preserve that distinction without filling the stage with implementation caveats.
