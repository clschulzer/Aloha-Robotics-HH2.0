# Design and evidence review — Night Shift

Source checkpoint: `5d41feb`, after the completed Christian-template version. GitHub was fetched before this build; there were no open pull requests or unseen commits.

## A separate interpretation

This version follows one evening rather than presenting three abstract pillars. It preserves the user's movie → character → serious planning → workflow → impact sequence, while changing the visual language:

- The first act reads like a clean message from an unusually needy colleague. The original character occupies the right half; its short message and actual device state occupy the left.
- The theatre board is horizontal. Theatre names, case blocks, time axis, the cancellation and emergency coverage establish a recognizable day.
- The golden window becomes a physical seed and travels to the service handoff. That same line continues through a human, KettyBot and a returned service record.
- The closing horizon suggests the next case without inventing a savings number or a success rate.

## Lessons applied from OPUS and Christian

OPUS makes a transition feel intentional by preserving the same visual material across scenes. Its best workflow animation also connects causality with arrival: the line reaches a node before it blooms. Night Shift uses these principles with fewer objects and no ornamental branches.

Christian's storyboard distinguishes priority from timing. A needy character tells us that care is overdue; clinical schedules and device use determine when that care can happen. The script preserves that distinction and gives a person the final decision.

The final sentence is a benefit, not a specification. Its preceding service receipt still leaves review with the responsible team.

## Evidence behind the story

The challenge-owner interviews identify switched-off mobile equipment, short connected periods, unclear responsibility, unsuccessful updates noticed late, and missing return logs. Downloading the package alone does not solve those problems.

The team's rule core is reused without changing its scheduling constants. The illustrated Cio window is calculated, not placed by a drawing coordinate. Arcus is a calendar-checked replacement. This is synthetic planning data, not a hospital forecast.

The physical connection, approved remote start and clinical release workflow require further integration and validation. Mechanical calibration is outside the autonomous concept.

## Iteration

The initial visual pass exposed an opacity-selector conflict that left a cancelled case visible over the result. The selector was narrowed to ordinary cases. Scout convergence was shortened and the winning label delayed slightly so motion clears before the result becomes readable. The phone and returned receipt were resized to avoid text crossing their frames. The final version was then rehearsed from the beginning.
