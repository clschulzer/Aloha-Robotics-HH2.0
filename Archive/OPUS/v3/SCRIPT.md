## If you’re running long
- Drop the bracketed words.
- Night: say only “22:00. KettyBot finds Bogdan, plugs in power and network, checks the area.” and “22:20: a human taps start.”

## Delivery
- **Light part:** quick and deadpan. The captions are the jokes; let them land.
- **The TV dies → “Now.” → the clock:** this is the turn. Silence, then the word, then let the clock tick for one second before you speak.
- **The night:** tell it like a thriller, with the times. Point at the clock once.
- **“Nobody noticed.”:** the most important line. Pause before it and after it.
