# Aloha Robotics · the 3-minute script

About 310 spoken words over 2:28, plus the 30-second film. **Bold** is what's on screen, normal text is what you say, and *italics* are stage directions. Words in [brackets] are optional: drop them if the presenter view shows you behind. The same script lives in `js/notes.js`, which feeds the presenter view (`P`), the script overlay (`N`) and the rehearsal autoplay (`?auto`).

A suggested split: **Speaker A** (warm, funny) does Act I and the outro. **Speaker B** (calm, precise) does Act II. One person can also do all of it. A third person on the clicker says nothing.

## If you're running long
- Drop the bracketed words (about 4 s).
- Swarm beat 3: say only "Golden window. No socket, so KettyBot brings power."
- Architecture beat 3: merge it with beat 2: "OR plan, roster, trauma alarm, and teamplay Fleet."
- Never cut the outro. It's the part they remember.

## Delivery notes
- **Act I is fast and light.** Let each face land before you speak; the slide pops first.
- **The cut is the most important second of the pitch.** The old TV dies, then silence, then the sentence. Don't rush it.
- **In Act II, point at the screen once per beat:** at the swarm, the gold, the checklist, the phone.
- In the outro, slow down by 20 %. Pause before "invisible" and before "We're Aloha Robotics".
