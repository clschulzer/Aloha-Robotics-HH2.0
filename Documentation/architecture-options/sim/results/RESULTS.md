## Reading it

- One device (C-08 "Otto", the shock-room C-arm) almost never gets a window the scorer accepts at P(undisturbed) ≥ 0.8. The rules refuse to gamble with it in every option, so it counts as an escalation. In real life, medical physics would swap in the reserve for one night.
- The team rule caps installs at 2 per night. That sets the speed limit, not the hardware. With 12 C-arms, no option can finish faster than 6 nights.
- "Today" is shown for scale only. The two inputs that drive it (`todayLeftOn`, `todayPress`) are guesses. Siemens can replace them with teamplay Fleet data.
- Cost figures are rough. The robot uses 2026 dealer list prices for a KettyBot Pro (US$11k–20k); everything else is an assumption. The sources are in `Documentation/architecture-options/00-reference-architecture.md`.
